Field notes

How to Play

Goal

Push north and secure Oasis Cut, Walled Mile, Blackwater Span, and The Redoubt. The Iron Citadel waits at the end: a war machine called the Bulwark. Free every prisoner in blue and walk them into a marked landing zone.

Touch

Stick moves. Hold FIRE to shoot the way you are heading — nearby enemies in that cone are aimed for you. GRENADE throws one grenade.

Keyboard and mouse

WASD or arrow keys move. You fire the way you face. Hold Space or J to shoot. G or K throws a grenade. Hold the mouse button to aim that shot. P pauses. M cycles the volume.

Weapons

Rifle — always with you. Steady shots.
Spread — a wide fan. Good up close.
Machine gun — fast, hungry for ammo.
Rocket — blows cover, nests, and bunkers.
Flame — a short cone of fire.
Grenades — arcing blasts. Your own will kill you.

Cover

Sandbags and crates stop rifle fire. Grenades and rockets break them. Barrels explode, and a chain of them slows time. Walls and buildings block your path. Tank traps stop you; bullets pass. Barbed wire and mud slow your walk. Only bridges cross water.

Enemies

Flank gun nests. Bunkers shrug off frontal fire — use explosives, or shoot in through the open back. Mortars paint a red circle: leave it. Shoot the tank on a flamer’s back to burst him. Rooftop snipers show a red laser before they fire. Trucks drop a squad unless you wreck them first.

Prisoners

Walk into a prisoner to free them. They follow you. They can be shot or caught in a blast, and they do not stop bullets. Lead them into a landing zone, or bring them through the gate, for an extraction bonus.

Difficulty

Easy, Normal, or Hard. The choice is remembered on this device. Lives and starting grenades change the next time you deploy. Easy is built for a casual push. Hard shoots sooner and straighter.
